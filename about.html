About Popi Solitaire

Popi Solitaire is a mobile solitaire built around emoticon tiles. Instead of classic suits, you match expressions, clear stacked layers, and ride the mood of each run — soft chaos, warm colors, and one more “just one level” vibe.

Genre · Solitaire / Puzzle Platform · Android & iOS Version · 0.1.0

How to play

Simple to learn, still sneaky when the board stacks up.

  1. 1 Pick free tiles from the board — uncovered faces, or slides that can escape to an open side.
  2. 2 Build matching sets on the tray. Matching clears space and unlocks the board underneath.
  3. 3 Watch your mood: keep it healthy through objectives and events to finish the level strong.

What you’ll find

  • Layered solitaire boards with emoticon tiles
  • Mood meter and seasonal-style gameplay events
  • Level progression with shifting objectives
  • Hints, undo, and soft juice when matches land
  • Music, SFX, and vibrate toggles in Settings
